How to play

  1. Deal. The deck is dealt out so everyone holds the same number of cards. If 52 doesn't divide evenly, the lowest cards are set aside first: A♠ A♥ A♦ A♣, then the 2s. Whoever holds the 7♥ opens the round.
  2. Build. On your turn, play a 7 to open a suit, or the next card above or below a run already on the table. With nothing playable, you pass.
  3. Finish. The round ends the moment someone plays their last card.

Scoring

Cards left in your hand count against you. Lowest total wins.
