How to Play & Scoring

All 52 cards are dealt out. Whoever holds the 7♥ opens the round. Play a 7 to open a suit, or the card directly above or below an active suit run.

The round ends the moment the first player plays their last card. Everyone else tallies their remaining cards: A = 1, 2–10 = face value, J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13. Lowest total score wins!
