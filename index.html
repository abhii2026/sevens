How to play

  1. Deal. The deck is dealt out so everyone holds the same number of cards. If 52 doesn't divide evenly, the lowest cards are set aside first: A♠ A♥ A♦ A♣, then the 2s. Whoever holds the 7♥ opens the round.
  2. Build. On your turn, play a 7 to open a suit, or the next card above or below a run already on the table. With nothing playable, tap Pass; after 5 seconds it passes for you.
  3. Finish. The round ends the moment someone plays their last card.

Scoring

Cards left in your hand count against you. Play as many rounds as you like; when the host taps End game, the lowest total across all rounds wins.
